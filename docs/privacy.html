Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how TelePort ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information collected from users of our application. TelePort is designed as a local connectivity tool for Android TV and mobile devices.

1. No Collection of Personal Information

We believe in absolute user privacy. TelePort does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information. This includes but is not limited to:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account details.
  • Location data or GPS coordinates.
  • Device identifiers (such as IMEI or MAC addresses).
  • Photos, files, contacts, or calendar data.

2. Screen Casting and Media Projection

To enable screen casting from your mobile device to your Android TV, the App utilizes Android's MediaProjection API. This process is executed entirely locally on your device and Wi-Fi network. No video or audio content captured during screen projection is ever stored by us, nor is it sent to external servers or third parties.

3. Local Network Data Transmission

TelePort facilitates connection between devices via your local area network (LAN) over Wi-Fi using Network Service Discovery (NSD) and WebSocket connections. All communication and data transfer (such as controller inputs, discovery signals, and media streams) occur directly between the paired mobile device and the TV. No network data goes through our or any external servers.

4. Camera Permission (QR Code Pairing)

The App may request access to your device's camera. This permission is used solely to scan the pairing QR code displayed on your TV screen to establish a connection. No images or video recordings are saved, stored, or transmitted.

5. No Third-Party Integration

We do not integrate any third-party SDKs, ad networks, tracking tools, or analytics platforms. There is no tracking of your activity across websites or other apps.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page with the updated effective date.
