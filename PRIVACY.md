# Privacy Policy for TelePort Cast & Remote

Last updated: 2026-06-02

## Overview
We take your privacy seriously. TelePort Cast & Remote is designed to operate securely and keep your data safe.

## What Data We Collect
**TelePort Cast & Remote does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, telemetry, or browsing history.**
All preferences and user configuration settings are stored strictly on your local device. Screen sharing and tab casting data are streamed directly over your local network to your TelePort TV receiver and are never sent to external servers or logged.

## How Data Is Stored
All data is stored locally on the device using standard Chrome Extension API methods:
- `chrome.storage.local`: Used to save settings, configuration, and device pairing codes.

No data is uploaded, shared, or synced to external servers.

## Third-Party Services
This extension does not use any third-party services, APIs, analytics platforms, or external tracking services.

## Contact
If you have any questions or feedback regarding this policy, please open a GitHub Issue in the project repository.
