# Privacy Policy for TelePort Cast & Remote

**Last Updated:** June 3, 2026

## Overview

TelePort Cast & Remote is a Chrome extension that allows you to cast and control media from your browser to TelePort-enabled devices on your local network. This privacy policy explains how the extension handles data.

## Data Collection

TelePort Cast & Remote does **NOT** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

### Local Network Communication

- The extension communicates only with TelePort devices on your **local network**
- All communication happens directly between your browser and local devices
- No data is sent to external servers or third parties

### Permissions

The extension requires the following permissions:

- **activeTab**: To detect media content on the current webpage
- **Local Network Access**: To discover and communicate with TelePort devices on your local network
- **Storage**: To remember your device preferences locally in your browser

All data stored by the extension remains on your device and is never transmitted externally.

## Third-Party Services

This extension does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the extension's manifest and documentation.
